# Doctoral students in Germany and the EU in 2023

Germany had a relatively high share of tertiary students studying for doctoral degrees in 2023. Doctoral students accounted for 6.2% of Germany's tertiary students, compared with an EU average of 3.8%.

Germany also had the highest number of doctoral students among EU countries in 2023, with 205,300 students at doctoral level. Spain had 97,200 doctoral students and France had 69,600.

Among EU countries, Luxembourg had the highest doctoral share of tertiary students at 12.7%. Germany followed at 6.2%, and Czechia had 6.1%.
