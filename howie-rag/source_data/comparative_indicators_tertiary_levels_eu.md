# Comparative indicators for tertiary levels in the EU

| Level | EU share of tertiary students in 2023 | Sex pattern |
|---|---:|---|
| Short-cycle tertiary courses | 7.5% | Women 49.5% |
| Bachelor's degrees | 59.3% | Women 54.1% |
| Master's degrees | 29.3% | Women 58.3% |
| Doctoral degrees | 3.8% | Women 49.4% |

The doctoral level had a smaller share of women than bachelor's and master's levels. Master's programmes had the highest female share among the four levels listed. Short-cycle tertiary and doctoral programmes had male majorities at EU level in 2023.
