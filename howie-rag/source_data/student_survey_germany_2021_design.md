# The Student Survey in Germany 2021: study design

The Student Survey in Germany 2021 is an integrated student survey. It brings together three previously separate survey lines: the Social Survey, the Student Survey, and the survey on studying with impairments.

The study is designed as a cross-sectional survey with a long-term character. It provides information on the living and study situation of students in Germany.

The data package is available through the FDZ-DZHW metadata portal. The documentation includes study information, instruments and method reports.
