# Germany: demography, vocational routes and tertiary entry

Germany experienced a 13% increase in the number of children aged 0 to 4 between 2013 and 2023. The number is projected to decline by 5% between 2023 and 2033.

In 2023, 90% of upper secondary vocational students in Germany were enrolled in programmes providing direct access to tertiary education. The OECD average was 77%.

Bachelor's or equivalent programmes are the main entry point into tertiary education in most OECD countries. On average across the OECD, 78% of those starting tertiary education for the first time enrolled in bachelor's or equivalent programmes. In Germany, the share was 82%.
