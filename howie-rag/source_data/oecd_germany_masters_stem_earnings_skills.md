# Germany: master degrees, STEM earnings and adult skills

In Germany, 15% of 25 to 34 year-olds held a master's or equivalent degree in 2024. The OECD average was 16%. Germany's share was 14% in 2019.

In Germany, tertiary-educated workers with a STEM qualification earned on average 10% more than tertiary-educated workers with a qualification in another field of study.

In Germany, 23% of adults aged 25 to 64 had literacy skills at or below Level 1 in 2023. The OECD average was 27%.

Tertiary-educated adults in Germany scored on average 37 points higher in literacy proficiency than adults with upper secondary or post-secondary non-tertiary attainment. The OECD average difference was 34 points.
