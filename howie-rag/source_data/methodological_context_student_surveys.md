# Methodological context for student surveys

Student surveys in higher education typically produce cross-sectional data about study conditions, student backgrounds, financing, employment, mobility, health, well-being, and study-related assessments.

The Student Survey in Germany 2021 is cross-sectional with a long-term character. EUROSTUDENT 8 is cross-national and comparative, covering 25 countries and more than 290,000 student responses.

Survey-based student data are suitable for describing distributions, associations and subgroup differences. Cross-sectional survey data do not by themselves establish causal effects without additional design assumptions or longitudinal information.
