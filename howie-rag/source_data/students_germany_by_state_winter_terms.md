# Students in Germany by federal state and winter term

The table gives total student counts at higher education institutions by federal state for the winter terms 2022/2023 to 2025/2026. The 2025/2026 values are first preliminary results.

| State | 2022/2023 | 2023/2024 | 2024/2025 | 2025/2026 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Germany | 2,920,263 | 2,868,311 | 2,864,122 | 2,876,938 |
| Baden-Württemberg | 354,699 | 351,259 | 350,293 | 353,321 |
| Bavaria | 403,435 | 405,492 | 409,615 | 404,320 |
| Berlin | 199,593 | 200,440 | 200,852 | 206,458 |
| Brandenburg | 50,427 | 51,468 | 52,671 | 54,608 |
| Bremen | 37,608 | 37,509 | 38,249 | 39,869 |

Germany recorded 2,876,938 students in winter term 2025/2026, compared with 2,864,122 in 2024/2025 and 2,920,263 in 2022/2023. Bavaria remained above 400,000 students in all four winter terms shown. Berlin increased from 199,593 students in 2022/2023 to 206,458 in 2025/2026.
