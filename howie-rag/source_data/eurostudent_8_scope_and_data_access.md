# EUROSTUDENT 8 scope and data access

EUROSTUDENT is a network of researchers, data collectors, national ministries and other stakeholders examining social and economic conditions of student life in European higher education systems.

In the eighth EUROSTUDENT round, survey responses were collected from more than 290,000 students in 25 countries across the European Higher Education Area.

The project covers access to higher education, study conditions, international student mobility and assessment of studies. The results are intended to inform policy debates and support further research.

The main EUROSTUDENT 8 report is the Synopsis of Indicators. Additional topical reports cover digitalisation, the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic, discrimination, and students' well-being and mental health. Data are available through the project database and as a Scientific Use File.
