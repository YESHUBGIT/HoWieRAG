# Germany: adult literacy change and training participation

Between Cycle 1 of the Survey of Adult Skills in 2012–2015 and Cycle 2 in 2023, average literacy scores in Germany increased by 3 points among adults with tertiary attainment, from 293 to 296. Over the same comparison, the score for adults without upper secondary attainment decreased by 19 points, from 222 to 203.

In Germany in 2023, 74% of adults aged 25 to 64 with high literacy proficiency at or above Level 4 participated in formal or non-formal education and training in the last year. Among adults with proficiency at or below Level 1, the participation rate was 23%.
