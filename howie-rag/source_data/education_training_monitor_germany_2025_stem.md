# Germany in the Education and Training Monitor 2025

The Education and Training Monitor 2025 country report for Germany identifies skilled labour shortages as a key challenge. Germany has very high participation in work-based learning in vocational education and training, but socially disadvantaged students continue to face difficulties accessing dual learning.

STEM subjects are popular in German tertiary education. Germany aims to increase the number of STEM specialists through the STEM Action Plan 2.0. The plan brings STEM closer to people across education stages, from early childhood education and care to adult learning.
