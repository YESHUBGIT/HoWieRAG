# Regional tertiary attainment in the EU in 2024

In 2024, 44.2% of EU residents aged 25 to 34 had tertiary educational attainment. Among 242 NUTS level 2 regions with available data, 84 had reached or surpassed the 45.0% EU policy target for tertiary attainment.

The regional pattern indicates that the EU average was close to the target, while a substantial minority of regions had already reached or exceeded it. The indicator refers to the 25 to 34 age group and includes tertiary educational attainment at ISCED levels 5 to 8.
