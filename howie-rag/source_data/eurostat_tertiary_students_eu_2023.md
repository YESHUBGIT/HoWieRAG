# Tertiary education students in the EU in 2023

In 2023, the EU had 18.8 million tertiary education students. Germany had 3.3 million tertiary education students, the highest number among EU countries and equivalent to 17.7% of the EU total. France accounted for 15.2% of the EU total, Spain for 12.6%, and Italy for 11.8%.

Distribution by level in the EU in 2023:

| Tertiary level | Share of tertiary students |
|---|---:|
| Short-cycle tertiary courses | 7.5% |
| Bachelor's degrees | 59.3% |
| Master's degrees | 29.3% |
| Doctoral degrees | 3.8% |

Bachelor's and master's students together accounted for 88.7% of total tertiary students in the EU in 2023.
