# Students at private higher education institutions in Germany

The table gives student counts at private higher education institutions in Germany by type of institution for winter terms 2022/2023 to 2024/2025.

| Type of higher education institution | 2022/2023 | 2023/2024 | 2024/2025 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Private institutions of higher education, total | 365,022 | 372,887 | 383,672 |
| Universities | 23,690 | 24,630 | 26,170 |
| Colleges of theology | 472 | 400 | 421 |
| Colleges of art | 1,810 | 1,988 | 1,794 |
| Universities of applied sciences | 337,522 | 344,318 | 353,794 |
| Universities of applied administrative sciences | 1,528 | 1,551 | 1,493 |

Private higher education enrolment increased from 365,022 in winter term 2022/2023 to 383,672 in 2024/2025. Universities of applied sciences accounted for the largest share, with 353,794 students in 2024/2025.
