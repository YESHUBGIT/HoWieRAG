# Limitations context for higher education statistics

Higher education statistics differ by source, reference year, population definition, reporting period and educational classification. National statistics for German winter terms and European statistics for calendar years are not always directly comparable.

Destatis winter-term student counts describe students at higher education institutions in Germany. Eurostat tertiary education statistics cover tertiary education students and use international education classifications. OECD indicators may refer to adults or young adults in specific age groups and often combine survey and administrative data.

Comparisons should preserve the original reference population, year, country group and classification. Values for 2025/2026 student counts from Destatis are preliminary.
