# Germany private higher education enrolment share estimate

In winter term 2024/2025, Germany had 2,864,122 students at higher education institutions. In the same winter term, private higher education institutions had 383,672 students.

Using these two figures, private higher education institutions accounted for approximately 13.4% of students in winter term 2024/2025.

Calculation: 383,672 divided by 2,864,122 equals approximately 0.13396.
