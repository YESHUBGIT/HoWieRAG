# Field distribution of tertiary students in the EU in 2023

| Field | Share of all tertiary students | Sex distribution detail |
|---|---:|---|
| Business, administration and law | 22.0% | Women were a majority |
| Engineering, manufacturing and construction | 14.7% | 72.3% male |
| Health and welfare | 14.4% | 72.4% female |
| Arts and humanities | 11.2% | 65.0% female |

Business, administration and law was the largest broad field of tertiary education. Engineering, manufacturing and construction was the second largest field and had a strong male majority. Health and welfare was the third largest field and had a strong female majority.
