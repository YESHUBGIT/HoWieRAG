# Germany: young adults, education and unemployment

Germany's share of young adults aged 25 to 34 without upper secondary attainment was 15% in 2024, above the OECD average of 13%.

In Germany, unemployment among economically active young adults aged 25 to 34 differed by educational attainment. The unemployment rate was 9.3% for young adults without upper secondary qualification, 3.3% for those with upper secondary or post-secondary non-tertiary attainment, and 3.5% for those with tertiary attainment.

Across the OECD, unemployment among economically active young adults aged 25 to 34 averaged 12.9% for those without upper secondary education, 6.9% for those with upper secondary or post-secondary non-tertiary education, and 4.9% for tertiary-educated young adults.
