# Sex and field patterns in EU tertiary education in 2023

Women accounted for 54.8% of all tertiary students in the EU in 2023. By level, women accounted for 49.5% of students in short-cycle tertiary courses, 54.1% in bachelor's degrees, 58.3% in master's degrees, and 49.4% in doctoral degrees.

The largest field of tertiary education in the EU in 2023 was business, administration and law, with 22.0% of all tertiary students. Engineering, manufacturing and construction accounted for 14.7% of tertiary students. Health and welfare accounted for 14.4%. Arts and humanities accounted for 11.2%.

Engineering, manufacturing and construction had a male majority: 72.3% of students in this field were male. Health and welfare had a female majority: 72.4% of students were women. Arts and humanities also had a female majority, at 65.0%.
