# Trend notes on students in Germany

Total student enrolment at German higher education institutions decreased from 2,920,263 in winter term 2022/2023 to 2,864,122 in winter term 2024/2025, then increased to 2,876,938 in winter term 2025/2026.

Private higher education enrolment increased in each observed winter term: 365,022 in 2022/2023, 372,887 in 2023/2024, and 383,672 in 2024/2025.

The strongest private-sector concentration by institution type was in universities of applied sciences. In 2024/2025, universities of applied sciences had 353,794 of the 383,672 students enrolled at private higher education institutions.
