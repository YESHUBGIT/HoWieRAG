# Tertiary educational attainment among young adults in the EU in 2024

In 2024, 44.2% of the EU population aged 25 to 34 years had a tertiary level of educational attainment. The EU policy target for this age group is 45.0%.

Among 242 NUTS level 2 regions with available data, 84 regions reached or surpassed the 45.0% target. Approximately half of women aged 25 to 34 years in the EU had tertiary educational attainment.
